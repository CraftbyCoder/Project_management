About

Coordination that keeps delivery on time.

I work at the intersection of requirements, teams and timelines. My day-to-day involves analysing FRDs and Figma designs, breaking work into trackable Jira tickets, preparing test cases and user guides, and keeping Confluence documentation clean enough that anyone can pick up the thread.

Across healthcare and hospitality projects, I've supported 12+ Agile sprints end-to-end — from daily stand-ups and sprint planning through reviews and retrospectives — while coordinating design, development and testing teams and documenting stakeholder decisions.

“Good coordination isn't loud. It's the reason things land on time.”

I'm now seeking a Project Coordinator, PMO Analyst or Project Support role where organised, on-time delivery matters as much as it does to me.
